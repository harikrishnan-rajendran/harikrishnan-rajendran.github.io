01 / RESEARCH

Research interests

How do individuals coordinate? How do collective patterns emerge, change and function?

01

Social insects

Collective organisation, division of labour, social immunity and decision-making in ant and bee colonies.

02

Collective behaviour

How local interactions, individual variation and feedback produce coordinated group-level dynamics.

03

Self-organisation & modelling

Mechanistic models and quantitative approaches to understand emergent spatial and behavioural patterns.

04

Ecology & movement

Animal movement, ecological interactions and the consequences of behaviour for biological systems.
